Educational Scope

Drug-drug interactions occur when the pharmacokinetics (ADME) or pharmacodynamics of one substance are altered by another.

  • Enzyme Inhibition/Induction: e.g., CYP3A4, CYP2C19 liver enzymes affecting metabolism rate.
  • Competitive Binding: Direct competition for receptor active sites or plasma proteins.
  • Synergistic Toxicity: Multiple agents acting on the same physiological pathway (e.g., dual anticoagulation).
  • Chelation & Absorption: Insoluble complex formation preventing intestinal uptake.
